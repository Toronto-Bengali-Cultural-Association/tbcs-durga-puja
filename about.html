Our Story

Bringing the spirit of Bengal to the GTA

[PLACEHOLDER] Toronto Bengali Cultural Society (TBCS) is a volunteer-run, not-for-profit organization dedicated to celebrating and sharing Bengali culture, language, and tradition with the Greater Toronto Area.

Who We Are

[PLACEHOLDER] Founded by a handful of families, grown into a community of hundreds

[PLACEHOLDER] TBCS began when a small group of Bengali families in Toronto decided the community deserved a Durga Puja that felt like home — complete with traditional rituals, home-style bhog, and a stage for local artists. What started as a modest gathering has grown into one of the GTA's cherished annual celebrations.

[PLACEHOLDER] Today, TBCS is run entirely by volunteers who dedicate months of planning to bring five days of Puja, performances, and community bonding to life each year.

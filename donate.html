Your Impact

Where Your Donation Goes

Community & Outreach

Supports bhog for all attendees and year-round community programming beyond Durga Pujo.

Venue & Rituals

Covers the hall rental, purohit, and all materials needed for daily pujo and aarti.

Cultural Programs

Funds the stage, sound, and performers for our dance, music, and drama programs.

For Businesses

Become a Sponsor

Sponsorship puts your business in front of hundreds of families across two days of celebration, and directly funds the Pujo.

Your Banner on Screen

Your artwork runs in the sponsor slideshow at the venue throughout both days, between cultural programs.

Reach the GTA Bengali Community

Durga Pujo draws families from across the Greater Toronto Area — a warm, engaged audience that returns every year.

Support a Not-for-Profit

Every sponsorship goes straight into the venue, rituals, and programming. TBCS is run entirely by volunteers.

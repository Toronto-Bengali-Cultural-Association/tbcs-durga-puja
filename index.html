Why join us

A Pujo built by family, for family

Every year TBCS volunteers come together to recreate the warmth of a hometown Durga Pujo, from Sandhi Pujo to Sindoor Khela.

Traditional Rituals

Pushpanjali, aarati, and the daily rituals performed by our purohit, open to all.

Cultural Programs

Dance, music, and dhunuchi naach performed by community members of all ages.

Community & Food

Bhog, food stalls, and plenty of room for adda with old friends and new.

Our Story

More than a Pujo, a community rooted in Toronto

Toronto Bengali Cultural Society exists to promote social justice and preserve our cultural heritage, creating a more harmonious and equitable society. Durga Pujo is our largest gathering, but our work runs year-round: religious harmony, cultural heritage, social welfare, and community partnership.

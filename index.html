Our Story

More than a Pujo — a community rooted in Toronto

Toronto Bengali Cultural Society exists to promote social justice and preserve our cultural heritage, creating a more harmonious and equitable society. Durga Puja is our largest gathering, but our work runs year-round: religious harmony, cultural heritage, social welfare, and community partnership.

This Year

Two days, one big celebration

Shashthi – Saptami

Saturday, Oct 10 — Shastir Bodhon, Adhibash & Shasti Bihit Puja, and the welcoming of Devi Durga.

Ashtami – Sandhi Puja

Sunday, Oct 11, 12:15 PM — the most sacred moment of the Puja, joining the end of Ashtami and start of Nabami.

Dashami – Visarjan

Sunday, Oct 11, 4–5 PM — Bishorjon and Sindoor Khela, the emotional farewell to Ma Durga.
